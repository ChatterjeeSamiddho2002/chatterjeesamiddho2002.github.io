About me

I'm an M.Tech student specializing in VLSI Design and Embedded Systems, with a strong focus on analog circuit design and simulation.

My primary interests lie in analog circuit design, circuit analysis, simulation, and analog CMOS layout. I'm particularly interested in understanding and designing transistor-level circuits — MOSFET-based circuits, small-signal analysis, analog amplifiers, differential pairs, current mirrors, cascode architectures, feedback and stability, op-amps, OTAs, and mixed-signal systems.

My technical experience spans multiple areas: Analog VLSI, Circuit Simulation & EDA (Cadence Virtuoso, SPICE/LTspice, Synopsys Sentaurus TCAD, MATLAB), and Embedded Systems (C, Embedded C, STM32, ARM-based microcontrollers, CAN/CAN FD, AUTOSAR).

01 / HEMT

Performance Optimization of InGaAs/AlGaAs HEMT Using Mole Fraction Engineering

Performed systematic parametric sweeps of Al and In mole fractions in Sentaurus TCAD. Extracted DC and analog performance metrics including drain current (I_D), transconductance (g_m), and intrinsic gain. Identified optimum heterostructure composition at Al = 0.30 and In = 0.20, achieving a peak intrinsic gain of 19.19 through enhanced 2DEG confinement.
